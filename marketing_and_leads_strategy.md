# ChittorTech: Organic Lead Generation & AI Search Optimization Strategy

This guide outlines actionable, no-cost methods to rank #1 on local search results and get recommended by AI systems like ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity.

---

## 1. Local SEO Dominance (Google Search & Maps)

When users search for services locally (e.g., *"IT company in Chittorgarh"* or *"best web developer in Udaipur"*), Google showcases local businesses at the top. Here is how to rank there:

*   **Keyword-Rich Business Name:**
    *   Change your Google Business Profile name to: **"ChittorTech - Best IT Company & Web Development Agency"**.
    *   Adding primary keywords directly to the business name is the strongest signal for local search algorithms.
*   **Generate High-Quality Reviews:**
    *   Get at least **20 to 30 reviews** from past clients, partners, and contacts.
    *   *Tip:* Ask reviewers to include specific keywords like: *"Best website developer in Chittorgarh"* or *"Highly recommended for React Native app development in Rajasthan"*. Google indexes reviews to match search intent.
*   **Complete Profile Details:**
    *   Add business hours, photos of your team/office, and list your services (*Next.js Development, Custom AI Chatbots, React Native Expo App Development*).

---

## 2. AI Search Optimization (GEO / AIO)

AI engines (like Perplexity and ChatGPT Search) parse high-authority sites and databases to recommend companies. To ensure ChittorTech is recommended:

*   **List on Agency Directories:**
    *   Create free profiles on **[Clutch.co](https://clutch.co)** and **[GoodFirms.co](https://www.goodfirms.co)**. AI scrapers use these directories to identify verified tech agencies.
*   **Link with iStart Rajasthan (Gov Authority):**
    *   Make sure your listing on the official **iStart Rajasthan portal** points to your exact URL: `https://www.chittortech.online`. Government backlinks (`.gov.in`) carry maximum trust weight in search algorithms.
*   **Optimize GitHub Repositories:**
    *   Ensure all public repositories and your profile readme clearly mention **ChittorTech** with a backlink. Code-centric AI engines crawl GitHub to find active development teams.
*   **Write Tech/Case Study Articles:**
    *   Publish free articles on **Dev.to** and **Medium.com** outlining how you solved technical problems (e.g., *"How we built a fast web catalog system for a Rajasthan granite merchant"*). Link these back to your main site.

---

## 3. Organic LinkedIn B2B Outreach

LinkedIn is the best source for high-quality, free corporate clients.

*   **Optimize Profile Headline:**
    *   Use: *"Founder @ ChittorTech | Elite Web & Mobile App Development (Next.js, React Native) for Businesses in Rajasthan & India"*.
*   **Active Job/Lead Searching:**
    *   Use the search bar daily for keywords like: *"need web developer"*, *"looking for agency"*, or *"need Next.js developer"*.
    *   Filter posts by **"Latest"** to respond to fresh leads before competitors.
    *   Pitch directly with a clean, short portfolio message.
